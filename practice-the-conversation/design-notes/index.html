Design Notes

!

This is not AI-adaptive learning.The experience uses authored branching paths, predefined rules and targeted feedback. It does not use AI to evaluate responses or generate new learning paths in real time.

02

The Logic

A response is checked against the criteria for that scenario. Its words and structure help determine skill ratings, feedback and the next authored prompt. The customer scenario has less branching than the other two.

How feedback is selected

What the system findsWhat the learner sees
Highest-rated skill“What worked” — specific positive guidance
Lowest-rated skill“Try next” — one focused improvement
All five skills rated at least 4 out of 5A more challenging next step

How scoring works

Each scenario tracks five skills. A response receives a rating for each skill, usually from 1 to 5. After the practice rounds, the system averages the ratings for each skill and rounds that average to a whole number. Each final skill rating is worth up to 20 points. Five skills together make a score out of 100.

Measurement + Research

What to measure

QuestionEvidence
Did learners engage?Completion, feedback usefulness, perceived relevance
Did skills improve during practice?First-to-final ratings & quality on unfamiliar scenarios
Did behavior change on the job?Observable conversation & decision-making behaviors
Did changes create business value?Relevant service, leadership or operational indicators

Data and reporting

The prototype can export scenario responses, round-by-round skill ratings, final scores, and reflections to CSV. A future dashboard could summarize practice completion, skill patterns and areas for improvement. Work outcomes would require separate data collection.
