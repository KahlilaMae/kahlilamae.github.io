Three-year growth plan

Use this illustrative plan to decide when the process is ready to expand.

  1. Year 1

    Pilot with one team

    The billing lead tests the backup approval path. Track the share of urgent credits resolved before cutoff and the hours spent on corrections. Use the first month as a baseline.

  2. Year 2

    Expand regionally

    Regional leads roll out the process to 200 staff after the pilot shows fewer late credits and an approval audit confirms that decisions are documented. Compare results across teams each quarter.

  3. Year 3

    Standardize nationwide

    Operations expands to 10,000 staff if regions sustain the gains. Review time saved alongside approval compliance each quarter and fix any step that still causes delays.

Decision for leadership

Approve a one-team pilot to resolve urgent credits before the payment cutoff. Track on-time resolution and test whether the renewal rate for affected accounts rises from an illustrative 85% baseline to 90%. Use the results to decide whether to expand regionally.

What needs to change

The $500 approval threshold gives managers oversight of larger credits. In this scenario, the team may miss tonight’s payment cutoff because the backup team hasn’t confirmed when it can review the request. A late credit can lead to a payment correction and another customer contact.

Where to grow

Clarify ownership. Make it clear how to flag a request at risk of missing the cutoff. Record the deadline, review status, and approval decision in the same ticket.

Connect your next step to a result. Review your response below. Who would you contact before the cutoff? What would you track to see whether your approach reduces rework while keeping approvals documented?

Sample manager follow-up

For urgent credits above $500, flag the payment deadline and follow up in the backup approvals queue to confirm who owns the review and when they expect to decide. Document the approval before applying the credit.
