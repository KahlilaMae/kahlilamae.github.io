I’m interested in how people learn, grow, and figure out what comes next.

My work has always centered on learning, leadership, and coaching. Technology has become another way I think about how to make that work better.

What Guides Me

I like taking an idea, pulling it apart, and figuring out what might work.

That curiosity has followed me through teaching, coaching, leadership, higher education, and now learning and technology.

The work has changed over the years, but what draws me to it hasn’t. I like helping people see something differently, figure out what’s possible, and make it happen.

My approach
01

I ask a lot of questions. Probably too many.

Usually because I’m trying to understand what’s actually going on before jumping to an answer.

02

I notice what’s happening.

I pay attention to who is in the room, who isn’t being heard, and what people need to move forward.

03

I like a good mess.

Give me a complicated idea, too many moving pieces, or something that isn’t quite working yet. I like figuring out how the pieces fit together.

04

I think about the person on the other side.

Whether I’m designing learning, facilitating a conversation, or partnering with a leader, I think about how the experience will actually feel.
