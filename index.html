I’m interested in how people learn, grow, and figure out what comes next. I bring learning, leadership, coaching, and technology together to create experiences that are useful, thoughtful, and worth people’s time.

What Guides Me

I’m curious about people: how they learn, what helps them grow, and what becomes possible when they have the right support.

That curiosity has shaped my career from teaching and instructional coaching to school leadership, higher education, and professional learning with technology.

The positions have changed, but the part I love hasn’t: helping people figure things out and move forward.

My approach
01

I ask better questions.

Not to challenge for the sake of challenging, but because better questions usually lead to better solutions.

02

I notice what’s happening.

I pay attention to who is in the room, who isn’t being heard, and what people need to move forward.

03

I create clarity.

I enjoy taking something messy or complicated and making it feel manageable.

04

I think about the person on the other side.

Whether I’m designing learning, facilitating a conversation, or partnering with a leader, I think about how the experience will actually feel.
