I’m curious about how people learn and what helps us grow.

That curiosity has shaped my work in learning, leadership, and coaching, and how I think about technology’s role in learning.

What Guides Me

I like working through ideas with people and figuring out how to make them work.

That curiosity has followed me through teaching, coaching, leadership, higher education, and now learning and technology. The work has changed over the years, but what draws me to it hasn’t. I like helping people see something differently, figure out what’s possible, and make it happen.

My approach

I ask a lot of questions. Probably too many.

Usually because I’m trying to understand what’s actually going on before jumping to an answer. I’d rather work in community than figure it out alone.

I notice what’s happening.

I pay attention to who is in the room, who isn’t being heard,
and what people need to move forward.

I like a good mess.

Give me something complicated or not quite working yet, and I’ll want to figure out what’s going on and how to make it better.

I think about the person on the other side.

Whether I’m designing learning, facilitating a conversation, or partnering with a leader, I think about how the experience will actually feel.

What I’m exploring

Learning + Technology

I’m always exploring how technology can make learning more useful and relevant.

Leadership Development

I’m deeply interested in what helps people grow, especially through change, and become more effective.

Adaptive Learning + AI

I’m exploring how AI can make learning platforms more responsive to each person and improve the return on experience.

Questions I’m still asking

What are our systems making easier? What are they making harder?

What should AI help us do better? What should always belong to people?

How could we make it better?
