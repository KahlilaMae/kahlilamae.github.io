My Work Adaptive Learning
What Guides Me

I like working through ideas with people
and figuring out how to make them work.

The work has changed over the years, but what draws me to it hasn’t.
I like helping people see something differently and then make the next steps happen.

My Approach

I ask a lot of questions.

Usually because I’m trying to understand what’s actually going on before jumping to an answer. I’d rather work in community than go it alone.

I notice what’s happening.

I pay attention to who's in the room, who isn’t being heard,and what people need to move forward.

I'm drawn to a puzzle.

Give me something complicated or not quite working, and I’ll want to figure out what’s going on and how to make it better.

I think about the person on the other side.

Whether I’m designing a learning experience, working through an idea, or partnering with someone, I think about how the experience will actually feel.

What I’m Focused On

People & Leadership

I’m deeply interested in what helps people grow, especially through change, and become more effective.

Learning & Performance

I’m always thinking about how technology can make learning more useful, accessible, and relevant.

AI & the Future of Work

I’m exploring how AI can make learning more responsive and whether that leads to better performance and a return worth the investment.

Questions I’m Still Asking

What are our systems making easier?  What are they making harder?

What should AI help us do better?  What should always belong to people?

How do we know it’s actually helping?
