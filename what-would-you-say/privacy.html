Privacy

Here’s what to know about your data when you use this prototype:

  1. When you submit — Your response and scenario information are sent through Cloudflare to OpenAI with your consent.
  2. Feedback — LLM feedback and scores are experimental, not validated measures of proficiency or workplace performance.
  3. Your responses — Responses stay on this page for your report. This prototype does not intentionally save them to an application database.
  4. Provider policies — Service providers may process and retain data under their own policies.
  5. Before you respond — Avoid personal or confidential information.
