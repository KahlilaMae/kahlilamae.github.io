Apple Professional Learning Specialist | Jun 2025 – Jul 2026

Apple

K-12 Public School District

Context. The district was expanding Apple technology across 25 schools. Educators brought different levels of confidence with iPad and Mac. The district wanted that access to support broader instructional goals.

Challenge

Access was only the beginning. Educators needed to feel confident using the technology in their everyday work—and see how it could support their instructional goals.

Contribution

I worked with district leaders, IT, and educators to understand where support was needed. Their priorities shaped the learning I designed.

Through workshops, coaching, and practical resources, I helped educators build confidence and bring iPad and Mac into their everyday work.

  • Needs Assessment
  • Learning Strategy
  • Facilitation
  • Technology Adoption
  • Executive Partnership
  • Evaluation

Key Impact

25 schools supported
97% client satisfaction
150+ learning experiences
661 Apple Teacher certifications

Assistant Principal | Aug 2021 – Jul 2023

Lowell High School

San Francisco Unified School District

Context. With 1,880 students depending on it, Lowell was navigating pandemic recovery, changing student needs, a contentious admissions debate, and budget pressures. Essential student services still had to run.

Challenge

Keep the school stable as conditions changed. Staff needed support, students needed services, and difficult decisions had to serve the school’s broader goals.

Contribution

I led schoolwide initiatives and worked with department and district leaders to keep essential work moving. I coached and evaluated department chairs and educators, using performance data and improvement plans to address gaps.

I developed the Student & Family Handbook to clarify policies and practices. As Acting Principal in Fall 2022, I oversaw school operations, staff, and organizational priorities.

  • Team Leadership
  • Coaching
  • Performance Management
  • Cross-Functional Partnership
  • Change Leadership

Key Impact

5 department chairs
40+ staff members coached and evaluated
1,880+ students supported

Teacher Development Coach | Aug 2018 – Jul 2021

Pathway to Teaching, Pipeline Program

San Francisco Unified School District

Context. San Francisco faced a teacher shortage, especially in special education, science, math, and other hard-to-fill areas. People already working in schools needed a more accessible path to licensure.

Challenge

Help aspiring educators become licensed teachers while building the knowledge, classroom skills, and support to improve students’ social and academic outcomes.

Contribution

I designed courses in Google Classroom and Blackbaud LMS for the district’s teacher development pipeline. Weekly observations, individualized feedback, SMART goals, and improvement cycles guided my coaching toward California licensure.

I also managed Migrant Education Summer School for 85 students and 4 teachers, and a Science Workshop Series for 46 students.

  • Coaching
  • Learning Design
  • LMS Design
  • Program Management
  • Continuous Improvement
  • Feedback

Key Impact

39 teachers coached
113 schools served
1,200+ students impacted

Education Coordinator | May 2007 – Jan 2010

San José State University, Research Foundation

CME Society, EOP&S Former Foster Youth Program

Context. Former foster youth were entering college without supports many peers could rely on. Staying enrolled meant finding housing, paying for basic needs, and knowing where to turn when something went wrong. For many, there was no family safety net.

Challenge

Help students stay enrolled and move toward graduation by connecting them with resources and support they couldn’t always count on elsewhere.

Contribution

I co-taught career development courses for more than 35 students. I also designed workshops, educational programs, and residential summer camps.

I co-created the inaugural National Blueprint for Success Conference, bringing stakeholders together to advance college-to-career pathways for foster youth.

  • Program Design
  • Curriculum Development
  • Stakeholder Engagement
  • Event Design
  • Career Development

Key Impact

1st annual national conference
12% → 50% college enrollment
76% persisted toward graduation

Adjunct Lecturer | 2021 – 2024

University Appointments

Reach University, Oxford Teachers College | San Francisco State University

Context. Working adults were preparing to become educators while balancing jobs, coursework, and classroom responsibilities. Tuition costs and the long path to a credential added to that pressure.

Challenge

Make learning accessible and practical so working adults could keep moving toward a degree and teaching credential.

Contribution

I designed and taught virtual courses at Reach University and hybrid courses at San Francisco State University. Topics included instructional strategies, disability, early intervention, and ethical and legal practices.

Using adult learning principles, I built content, activities, and assessments in Canvas and iLearn. Feedback supported students as they developed as educators.

  • Adult Learning
  • Learning Experience Design
  • Course Development
  • Assessment Design
  • Canvas
  • iLearn

Key Impact

2 universities 2 learning platforms
5 courses taught
200+ educators
